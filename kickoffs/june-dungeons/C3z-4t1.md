   `UI_QUEST_NEW_CUSTOM_MSG`, `PARTY_UI_CUSTOM_MSG`, `LOCAL_MAP_UI_CUSTOM_MSG` and `DUNGEON_ID` members. `luabind.py check TutorialGuideInfo`
   (binder + enum gate) must PASS before the pack; a member March lacks is registered from June's Enum (no literals). Log "loaded N
