**Skill check, for every row of every table:** in a dungeon press the key near a monster. Cast 1: skill plays, cut-in shows, MP drops. Cast 2 at once: refused (cool time). Cast 3 after the clock: plays, MP drops again. You must be able to move afterwards (stuck = name the skill). `/mpf` every 2-3 skills.
